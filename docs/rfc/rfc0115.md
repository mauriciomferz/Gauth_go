# RFC 0115 (Placeholder Extract)

## 1. Power of Attorney Structure
A Power of Attorney (POA) document MUST enumerate principal, delegate, scope and validity period.
It SHOULD include formal requirements enabling external verification.

## 2. Scope Semantics
Scope definitions MUST support hierarchical narrowing.
Implementations SHOULD allow pattern or range expressions for advanced narrowing.

## 3. Validity Period
POA artifacts MUST define `valid_from` and `valid_until` timestamps.
Verification logic SHOULD consider reasonable clock skew.

## 4. Formal Requirements
Formal requirements MAY include notarization references and multi-signature attestations.

## 5. Power Limits
Quantitative limits MUST be enforceable (e.g., maximum transaction value, daily quota).

## 6. Rights & Obligations
Rights granted and obligations imposed SHOULD be serialized canonically and hashed.

## 7. Special Conditions
Special conditions MAY specify exceptional overrides subject to stricter audit.

## 8. Joint Signatures
Multi-party representation MUST support threshold semantics (M-of-N) with aggregate verification.

## 9. Canonical Serialization
Canonical POA digest MUST be stable under field reordering and extraneous whitespace removal.

## 10. Revocation Semantics
Revocation entries MUST immediately invalidate derived delegations.
Revocation chains SHOULD be externally anchored.
